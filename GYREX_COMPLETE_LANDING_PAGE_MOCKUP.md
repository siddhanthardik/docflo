2. **Engine 2: 5×5 Geo-Rank Heatmap Tracker** (Blue Accent Border)

## 📱 Mobile-First Native App Design Principles

1. **Card-Based UI**: Styled like native iOS/Android healthcare apps with soft `rounded-2xl` borders and touch-friendly padding.
2. **Bottom Action Bar on Mobile**: Sticky bottom CTA bar on mobile screens containing `[Sign In]` and `[Free Audit]` for maximum mobile conversion.
3. **Touch-Optimized Tap Targets**: Minimum 48px height on all buttons for effortless thumb navigation.
